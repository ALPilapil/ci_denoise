# Lab Meeting Slide Presentation Structure

All future slide decks, presentations, and research updates prepared for the PI and lab meetings MUST follow this 4-part progression:

1. **Summary of Last Time:**
   - Previous status, historical baseline, and context/feedback from the prior meeting.
   - What questions were left open and what was agreed upon previously.

2. **Brief Overview of What Was Done This Time:**
   - Concise summary of work completed during the current period.
   - New experiments run, algorithms implemented, datasets processed, or bugs resolved.

3. **Visualizations & Demo:**
   - Electrophysiological data displays (topographies, ERP waveforms, PSD spectra).
   - Quantitative leaderboards and comparison tables.
   - Interactive demos, single-subject/cohort case studies, and artifact rejection proofs.

4. **Up Next:**
   - Concrete next steps and immediate experimental priorities.
   - Strategic roadmap, timeline, and decisions required from the PI.
